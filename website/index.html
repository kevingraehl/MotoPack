MotoPack Lithium Battery Builder

Measure a battery box, or start with the voltage and capacity you want. For a sloped lid, stepped floor or tight corner, draw the inside outline and enter the width across it. Watch the cells fill the shape. Building a motorcycle? Place the motor in the frame before you size the pack.

Build a regular batteryPlan a full moto conversion layout

Choose the cells

Series groups set the voltage. The number of cells working together in each group sets capacity. The wizard shows both as you go and keeps each parallel group together in one bank.

Check the fit

Turn the cells, stagger the rows, add insulation and reserve room for your battery management board. Rotate the model and fade the case to inspect the clearances.

Take it to the bench

Review the nickel layout by bank and face. Download outlines or 3D parts for a test fit, and save the build for your next revision. Check the physical fit and electrical design before fabrication.
